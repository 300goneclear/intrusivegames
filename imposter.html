How it works: Everyone playing registers first. Once the round starts, everyone gets the same secret word — except one random imposter, who gets nothing and has to bluff. Talk it out without saying the word outright. After 6 minutes, voting opens — vote who you think is faking it. Once everyone's voted, the most-voted player is eliminated and a new 6-minute round begins. This repeats until the imposter is caught, or only 2 players remain and the imposter escapes. ⚠ Don't click through other people's names to peek at their word or role — only view your own. Keep your word and suspicions to yourself until voting.
